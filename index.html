Aira — AI Voice Companion

A warm, empathetic companion that listens, detects emotion, remembers what matters to you, tracks your mood, and responds like a caring friend who understands Indian culture, family pressure, homesickness and NRI life.

⚡ This browser demo uses the same emotion, crisis, memory and mood agents as the app, with rule-based replies. The full app runs offline with Whisper speech-to-text and the Ollama phi3:mini LLM.

How it works

  • Voice activity detection records you only while you speak
  • Whisper transcribes the audio offline
  • Emotion is detected with keyword scoring and a TextBlob sentiment fallback
  • Crisis language triggers a safe response with the 988 Lifeline
  • Memory and mood are injected into the LLM prompt, and the reply is streamed and spoken
